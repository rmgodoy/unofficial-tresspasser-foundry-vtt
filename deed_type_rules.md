INNATE: An innate deed requires no specific weapon
or implement to attempt. You can always attempt an
innate deed, no matter what you're holding in your
hands.
MELEE: A melee deed requires a free hand or a melee
weapon in hand, such as a sword or hammer. Most
melee weapons have a range of 1 square, meaning they
can be used to attempt deeds against adjacent
creatures only. A few weapons, such as spears, have a
melee range of 2 squares.
MISSILE: A missile deed requires a missile weapon in
hand, such as a crossbow or sling. When attempting a
missile deed, you can only target creatures and areas
within that weapon's range.
SPELL: A spell deed requires either a free hand or a
spell weapon, such as a wand or staff. When using a
free hand, your range is 4 squares. When using a spell
weapon, you can target creatures and areas within that
weapon's spell range instead.
TOOL: To perform a tool deed, you must have a free
hand, which you use to deploy a special tool. Tool
deeds have variable range, but usually it's equal to your
throwing range, 5 + AGILITY. The tool used for the
deed does not take up space in your inventory.
VERSATILE: A versatile deed can be attempted with
either a melee weapon or a missile weapon. If the
weapon you're using has both types, like a throwing
dagger, you can choose whether to attempt the deed as
a melee deed or a missile one. When you attempt a
versatile deed, it counts as the chosen type for the
purposes of talents and other effects. All characters
have the weapon attack deed by default, which is
versatile.
